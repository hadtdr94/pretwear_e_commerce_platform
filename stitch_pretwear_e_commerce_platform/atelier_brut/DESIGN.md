## Brand & Style

The brand occupies the intersection of avant-garde North African streetwear and haute-atelier personalization. Rooted in Algiers' architectural juxtaposition of French colonial haussmannian stone, Mediterranean brutalism, and contemporary youth culture, the aesthetic communicates uncompromising luxury, modern craftsmanship, and cultural authority.

The visual direction employs a High-Contrast Minimalist Luxury framework with editorial tension. Layouts prioritize deliberate negative space, architectural grid alignment, razor-sharp geometric framing, and oversized typographic scale. Tactile weight is achieved through stark tonal contrast between deep obsidian voids and gallery-grade warm stone backdrops, punctuated selectively by warm brass hardware tones.

## Colors

The palette references brutalist architectural materials, raw textile fibers, and brass atelier details:

- **Primary (`#111111`)**: Deep obsidian black. Serves as the principal structural color for heavy typography, hairline borders, structural framing, and high-impact action surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure optic white. Reserved for crisp product cutouts, high-contrast reverse type, and floating surface modules against alabaster canvas zones.
- **Neutral (`#F7F7F5`)**: Warm gallery alabaster/ecru. The default ambient canvas tone, providing physical presence and warmth distinct from clinical digital whites.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#B89B72`)**: Refined sand brass. Applied sparingly to indicate personalization affordances, monogram previews, gold-foil embroidery signifiers, and curated editorial badges.

Supporting neutral tiers:
- **Dark Grey (`#292929`)**: Secondary typography, metadata, and dark modal surfaces.
- **Light Grey (`#E8E8E8`)**: Hairline borders, structural dividers, and neutral state boundaries.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy enforces an editorial tension between expressive raw headlines and technical, modular product data:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles**: Rendered in Syne with tight letter-spacing. Primary campaign slogans and category callouts utilize all-caps styling with condensed vertical rhythm.
- **Body & Continuous Reading**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans for balanced legibility against both `#F7F7F5` and `#111111` containers.
- **Labels, Technical Specs & Pricing**: Set in Space Grotesk. All uppercase labels require wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).
- **Currency & Pricing**: Algerian Dinar (DZD) values must be formatted using Space Grotesk with bold weights (`price-display`), rendering the ISO code as a secondary superscript or inline label (e.g., `14,500 DZD`), keeping numbers prominent and unclipped across viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a fluid, structurally bounded 12-column system designed around high-impact editorial imagery and modular grid compartments:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid, `3.5rem` canvas margins, `2.5rem` gutters. Product showcases leverage asymmetrical spans (e.g., 7-column editorial campaign visual paired with a 5-column sticky configurator / personalization panel).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid, `2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Content reorganizes into paired 4-column modules.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column grid, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Personalization flows pin to bottom sheets with fixed-action summaries.

Horizontal dividing lines follow precise `1px` solid `#E8E8E8` or `#292929` rules that run flush to viewport margins, reinforcing the architectural atelier feel.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects conventional diffuse drop shadows in favor of editorial planar layering, solid offset drop-planes, and hairline borders:

- **Ground Level**: `#F7F7F5` base canvas.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` planes framed by crisp `1px` solid `#E8E8E8` borders.
- **Layer 2 (Overlays & Drawers)**: Deep `#111111` or `#FFFFFF` sheets using a razor-sharp `1px` perimeter border. When elevation separation is required, employ a crisp hard-edge drop: `0px 4px 0px #111111` instead of blurred ambient shadows.
- **Tactile Material Accents**: Subtle inset borders on active/selected items (`inset 0 0 0 1.5px #B89B72`) denote bespoke personalization states without shifting layout dimensions.
- **Backdrop Dimming**: Modal scrims utilize `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.75)` with a subtle blur (`4px`) to focus attention on configuration steps.

## Shapes

The geometry is uncompromisingly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

All corners for buttons, input modules, visual containers, product tiles, badges, and modals are strictly `0px` border-radius. This architectural precision reflects raw architectural street geometry and precise textile pattern cutting. The only circular geometry permitted across the system is strictly functional: color-swatch circles, thread-spool indicators for embroidery selection, and radio selection dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#111111` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0px` radius, `1px solid #111111`. Space Grotesk uppercase lettering (`label-lg`), `0.1em` tracking. Padding: `1rem 2rem`. Hover: `#B89B72` background with `#111111` text.
- **Secondary Action**: `#FFFFFF` background, `#111111` text, `1px solid #111111`. Hover: `#111111` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Atelier Accent (Personalize/Customize)**: `#B89B72` background, `#111111` text, `1px solid #B89B72`. Hover: `#111111` background with `#B89B72` text.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp rectangular containers. Padding: `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- Standard tags: `#F7F7F5` background, `1px solid #E8E8E8`, `#111111` text.
- Personalization / Limited Edition tags: `#111111` background, `#B89B72` text, `1px solid #B89B72`.

### Product Cards
- Seamless `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F7F5` structural container bounded by `1px solid #E8E8E8`.
- Aspect ratio `3:4` or `4:5` vertical fashion framing.
- Image area: clean cut out or editorial model photography. Hover displays immediate alternate angle or tactile personalization close-up (embroidery / print detail).
- Card footer: Syne headline, Space Grotesk size availability chips, and bold DZD currency text aligned baseline right.

### Input Fields & Personalization Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E8E8E8` border, transition to `1px solid #111111` on focus, and `1px solid #B89B72` when live previewing custom text. Zero border radius. Plus Jakarta Sans input text with Space Grotesk floating upper labels.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square geometric frames (`18px x 18px`), `1px solid #111111`. Checked state filled with solid `#111111` containing an interior sharp `#FFFFFF` check or inner square.

### Personalization Configurator Bar
- Sticky panel featuring a tactile stage indicator (Base Garment -> Placement -> Technique -> Preview).
- Technique selector chips toggle between "Silk Screen", "High-Density Puff", "Direct-to-Film", and "Gold Thread Embroidery", updating live price differentials dynamically in prominent `DZD`.
